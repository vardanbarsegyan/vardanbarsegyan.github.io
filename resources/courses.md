## General data analysis

- [Data Analysis for Social Science](https://scholar.harvard.edu/ellaudet/dss-instructor-materials), Elena Llaudet.
- [Modern Regression](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~cshalizi/mreg/15/), Cosma Shalizi.
- [Data Analysis and Politics](https://gov51.mattblackwell.org/), Matthew Blackwell and colleagues.
- [Quantitative Social Science Methods I](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/gov2001), Gary King and colleagues; [video lectures](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCtrwX29xpuWc9y0-0PKrHSQ/videos).
- [Regression analysis with Stata](https://www3.nd.edu/~rwilliam/stats3/index.html), Richard Williams.
- [Tidy Modeling with R](https://www.tmwr.org/), Max Kuhn and Julia Silge.
- [Modern Statistics for Modern Biology](https://www.huber.embl.de/msmb/index.html), Susan Holmes and Wolfgang Huber.
- [Computational Text Analysis](https://cjbarrie.github.io/CTA-ED/course-overview.html), Christopher Barrie.
- [Improving Your Statistical Inferences](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/), Daniël Lakens.
- [Linear Regression: A Visual Introduction](https://mlu-explain.github.io/linear-regression/), Jared Wilber.
- [Sociology Statistics Reading Group](https://scholar.princeton.edu/bstewart/sociology-statistics-reading-group), Brandon Stewart.
- [Advanced Social Statistics](https://scholar.princeton.edu/bstewart/bstewart/soc504-advanced-social-statistics), Brandon Stewart.
- [Statistics 110: Probability](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/stat110), Joe Blitzstein.
- [An Introduction to Political and Social Data Analysis Using R](https://bookdown.org/tomholbrook12/bookdown-demo/), Thomas Holbrook.
- [Awesome Computational Social Science](https://github.com/gesiscss/awesome-computational-social-science), a community-maintained collection.

## Causal inference

- [Causal Inference for the Brave and True](https://matheusfacure.github.io/python-causality-handbook/landing-page.html).

## Structural equation modelling

- [Lavaan e-learning](https://github.com/kylelang/lavaan-e-learning), Kyle Lang. Topics include factor analysis, multiple groups, mediation, moderation, missing data, and model selection.

## Multilevel modelling

- [Introduction to Multilevel Modelling](https://www.learn-mlms.com/), Mairead Shaw and Jessica Flake.
- [Multilevel modelling online course](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/cmm/learning/online-course/), University of Bristol Centre for Multilevel Modelling, with R, Stata, and MLwiN materials.

## Survival and event-history analysis

- [Survival Analysis in R](https://www.emilyzabor.com/tutorials/survival_analysis_in_r_tutorial.html), Emily Zabor.
- [Survival Analysis in R and Stata](https://grodri.github.io/survival/index), Germán Rodríguez.
- [Interaction effects in Cox regression](https://www.pauldickman.com/software/stata/parameterising-interactions/), Paul Dickman.

## Bayesian statistics

- [Bayesian Statistics](https://bayesf22.classes.andrewheiss.com/), Andrew Heiss and Meng Ye.

## Machine learning

- [CS229: Machine Learning](https://cs229.stanford.edu/), Stanford University.
- [Machine Learning and Causal Inference](https://github.com/Mixtape-Sessions/Machine-Learning), Brigham Frandsen.
- [Learning from Data](https://work.caltech.edu/telecourse), Yaser Abu-Mostafa.

- [Visualising Data](https://visualisingdata.com/book/).
